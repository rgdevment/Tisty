# Terms of use

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

## The short version

Tisty is free software you run on your own computer. There is no account, no
subscription and no service of ours in between: your notes, documents and tasks
stay on your disk, or in a place you choose. You may use it for anything, at
home or at work. It comes with no warranty, and keeping your data safe — a
backup, a sync you trust — is in your hands.

## What these terms cover

The Tisty desktop app for Windows and macOS, the `tisty` command that comes
inside it and opens the door for assistants, the plugins and the `tisty-mcp`
launcher that connect assistants to it, and the website www.tisty.app.

A service to share documents by link is planned. It is not covered here, and
will have terms of its own before it opens.

## Your data, your choices

- What you write in Tisty is yours. We claim no right to it, and we cannot
  see it.
- Keeping it safe is up to you. Tisty writes an append-only history and offers
  backups, but a disk that fails, a folder you delete or a sync you turn off
  can still lose work. Keep a backup somewhere else.
- When you sync through a folder, Google Drive, OneDrive or a WebDAV server,
  your copies live with that provider, under its terms.

## Assistants

If you connect an AI assistant, Tisty lets it file tasks and write documents
through MCP, within the limits described in the
[documentation](https://www.tisty.app/docs/). It never closes or deletes
anything on its own: when it says a task is done, you decide. What the
assistant reads is sent to whoever runs it, under that provider's terms. You
choose which assistants to connect, and you can close the door to all of them
in Settings › Assistants.
