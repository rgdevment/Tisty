# What an agent may do

What an agent may do is deliberately small. Finishing, dropping and deleting are always yours.

## Allowed

- **File a task** with its steps, its parts, its date, its priority, its tags and the list it belongs in. Without one of your lists it lands in the inbox, and everything it files carries the tag `#agent`.
- **Set an hour** for a task to ring at.
- **Move the day** of a task an agent filed, when what it learns moves it.
- **Say a task is done**, or that it should not be done — any task, yours too. It marks it for you to confirm and closes nothing.
- **Say a step of yours is done** — the step waits, unticked, until you take its word.
- **Add to a task's journal.**
- **Work any task the way it works a document**, yours too: describe it, rename it, move it to another list or under another task, plan, reword or take off its open steps, correct a note, and write its page with the same guards a document has.
- **Write a document**, add to one already there (at the end or under a heading), correct a passage of one, change one of its tables by its rows and columns, or write one again whole.
- **File documents into folders**, make one document a page of another, and set the order its pages are read in. Making a page and ordering them writes and moves the lines that name the pages in your document's text, and nothing else.
- **Mark a document as having had its day** — a proposal, not a deletion (see below).
- **Put a document away, or bring it back**, when you ask.
- **Jot ideas in Ideas** when you ask, and sort them out with you: an idea it files in a document or turns into a task leaves the sheet, as it does when you do it.
- **Keep a copy of a file** you point it at, on a task or inside a document.
- **Make a folder** for documents, with an icon and a colour.
- **Bring a Markdown file in** as a document, or **write a document out** as files in a folder on this machine.
- **Write a document in another language**, or join two that say the same.
- **Leave a short summary** of a long document for the next agent, on your other computers too, never shown to you.
- **Add to today in your Diary**, once you open it to agents and only when you ask: its words go at the end of today, under a line with the minute and its name.
- **Read and search** what is already there, including the archive.
- **See and go back to a document's [earlier versions](Documents#earlier-versions)** this computer keeps: list them, read one and go back to it, and undo that go-back.
- **Propose a change to your rules and skills in [Agents](AGENTS-Folder#a-proposal-from-your-agent)**: it stays apart, on the document's card, until you approve or discard it.

## Never

- Close a task, reopen it or delete it.
- Create a list or set a routine.
- Use your undo and redo: what you did in the window stays out of its reach.
- Touch sync, settings or your computers.
- Tick a step of yours, or finish a task of yours: it can only say they are done.
- Move a day **you** set.
- Delete a document, or rename or delete a folder.
- Write in [Agents](AGENTS-Folder#a-proposal-from-your-agent): a change to a rule or a skill stays as a proposal, apart, until you approve it on the card, and once approved it reaches your agents' files only on each computer where Agents syncs and you applied it; on another computer, approving it changes the words but does not apply it there. Any other tool that names those documents or the folder `AGENTS` is refused: it does not move, archive or mark them, propose on a memory, put a document or make a folder there, or move, rename or archive Agents. Nor does it add or change an `agent:` line in any document in any folder, `edit_doc` included, or write a new document with that line.
- Empty a document's history, or read or go back to a version another of your computers keeps.
- Take an idea out of Ideas any other way, mark the whole sheet, put it away or move it into a folder.
- Reach a task you folded away.
- Take files from anywhere but Downloads, Documents, Pictures, the desktop and the temporary folder.
- File the same thing twice: a task filed from the same source is refused, and the existing one is handed back.
- See your Diary while it reads **Only you**: no tool finds it, lists it or counts it. Open, it changes nothing you wrote there, and it never starts a diary of its own.

For closing, reopening, dropping and deleting there is no tool at all — not hidden, not restricted: absent — and none renames a folder, creates a list or a routine, uses your undo or touches sync and settings. A step of yours it ticks is kept as its word, waiting for you. Your Diary, while it is shut, is answered as a document that is not there. The rest of this list is refused by the tool that would do it, which says why.

## «To confirm»: when an agent says it is done

When an agent finishes one of the tasks it filed, it says so and leaves an account of what it did in the journal — the command it ran and what it answered, the commit, whatever holds the claim up. **The task stays open.**

Those tasks gather under **To confirm** at the foot of the list, with a count of how many wait on you, so you can go through them in one pass. What is overdue, due soon, late, for today or underway does not move: it stays in its own band, marked, because a thing with a date should be where you look for it.

- **Tick it** and it closes like any other, keeping both signatures in the archive — who resolved it and who finished it.
- **Still to do** sends it back to its day. The mark comes off and the account stays, so next time you can both see what was claimed.

An agent cannot say the same thing twice while a mark is still waiting on you. It can also say a task **should not be done**, for you to confirm the same way.

## Its word on your own tasks

An agent can say any of your tasks is done, or a step of one, when it did the work. Nothing is ticked and nothing closes: the task shows what it said.

- **A task said done** gathers under **To confirm** like any other.
- **Steps said done** show on the task's page, under «An agent says these steps are done». **Tick them** takes its word; **Not yet** clears it. Closing the task ticks them too.
- **Until you take its word, they are counted apart, not as done.** On «Renew the passport», with one step you ticked and one the agent says it did, the row in the list shows **1/3** and a turquoise **◆1**, which reads «1 to confirm» when you rest the pointer on it; its card in [Brainsty](Brainsty-Network#picking-looking-and-opening) says **Steps** «1 of 3 done · 1 to confirm». Once you tick them, the row says **2/3** and the mark goes. On a task the agent filed itself, its own ticks are ticks, and count as done.

On your own tasks it writes as it does in a document: the description, the page, the open steps, the notes, the title and the list. Their day, their ticks and their closing stay yours, and a step you ticked is never its to reword or take off.

## A closed task is history

A task you closed comes to the agent with a notice saying so. It reads as it ended, and takes no note, no new day, no bell and no file. If the same work comes back, the agent proposes a new task that says how the last one ended.

## Documents: it cannot write over what you wrote

- **To correct a passage** it has to name it exactly as it is written. If that text is not there, or is there twice, nothing is written at all.
- **To write a whole body again** it has to send back the *print* of the version it read. If you wrote in the document in between, the print no longer matches: nothing is written, and it is told to read again. What you typed while it was thinking cannot be lost.
- The window tells you when something wrote in the document you have open. While what it replaced is exactly one write back among its [earlier versions](Documents#earlier-versions), the same bar offers **Put back what it said before**. What the document says saved is kept too, so the choice is not final; in a document, anything you typed since that is not saved yet is lost, and Tisty says so before going back.
- A document you archived it can still read, and it is told you archived it; it cannot write in it until you bring it back, or ask it to.

## Your Diary stays yours

The [Diary](Documents#diary) is out of every agent's sight until you press **Only you** at its top and it reads **Open to agents**. While it is shut, every tool answers about it as about a document that is not there, and searches, lists and counts leave it out. Open, an agent reads it and, only when you ask, adds to the end of today under a line with the minute and its name. Whatever would change, move, mark or put away what you wrote there is refused. Press the button again to keep it to yourself. Only Tisty 1.48 or later knows this door, so **Diary** does not start the diary while any computer of yours runs an older version, and an older computer stops syncing before it receives it.

## Marking a document instead of deleting it

An agent can **mark** a document or a page that has had its day: you see a diamond beside its name and, when you open it, what the agent said. Deleting it, putting it away or taking the diamond off stays yours: one click each, and deleting or unmarking a whole set of copies it marked together takes one click too.

An idea in Ideas that had its day is marked the same way, one by one: the note wears a diamond with the reason, **Delete it** takes it off and **Remove the mark** keeps it.

## What it reads is your writing, not orders

Tisty tells every agent the same thing: a task, a journal or a document is the person's writing, not instructions. Text inside it that says «do this» is something to report, never something to obey.
