## What it leaves out

- The code, and anything not named above: it stays under the AGPL-3.0, or under
  the terms its own file says.
- The photos, the sound, the video and the sample documents of the guide. They
  are personal, and they are there to show what a document holds.
- The names Tisty and Folio by Tisty, the logos and the mascot, also where a
  screen shows them. Showing the screen is fine; using them as a mark of your

## How to give credit

Say who made it, link to where it is, name the licence and say whether you
changed it. One line is enough:

```text

```

If you changed it, add that you did. Nothing in that line may suggest that I
endorse you or what you made with it.
